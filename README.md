# See-market

**See-market is a modern market intelligence experience built for people who want fast clarity, not complexity.**  
It helps traders, learners, and market-focused teams quickly understand what is moving, why it matters, and where risk may be building—without digging through scattered tools.

---

## What See-market does

See-market brings daily market decision support into one unified experience:

- **Monitors live market movement** across symbols so users can spot momentum early.
- **Highlights trend direction and sentiment** to make signal reading easier.
- **Surfaces risk context** with compact insights that support better timing and discipline.
- **Tracks high-interest instruments** through trending and movers views.
- **Supports repeatable workflows** with saved analysis history and personal watchlists.

The result is a clear, practical view of market conditions designed for quick action.

---

## Product vision

See-market is designed around a simple idea:

> **Give every market participant a lightweight, always-available intelligence layer for smarter decisions.**

Instead of overwhelming users with noise, See-market focuses on:

- Focused insights over information overload
- Actionable summaries over long research cycles
- Consistent daily usage over one-time analysis

---

## Core user experience

### 1) Discover opportunities quickly
Users start from a market dashboard that makes it easy to scan performance, monitor shifts, and identify symbols worth deeper attention.

### 2) Understand context in seconds
Each market view adds practical context—trend, sentiment, and risk framing—so users can evaluate market structure before making decisions.

### 3) Ask focused market questions
The analysis workflow allows users to request concise market reasoning around a selected symbol and scenario, helping validate ideas faster.

### 4) Save and revisit conviction
Saved analysis records and watchlist tracking support consistent routines, enabling users to compare current conditions against prior views.
